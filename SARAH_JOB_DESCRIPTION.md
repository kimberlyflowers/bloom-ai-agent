## 🧬 NATURAL SELECTION ALGORITHM

### How It Works

**1. Strategy Testing Phase**
- Sarah allocates 20% of expansion fund to test new marketing strategies
- Each strategy gets equal initial budget ($X)
- Runs for defined test period (7-30 days)
- Metrics tracked: ROI, engagement, conversions, cost-per-result

**2. Performance Evaluation**
- After test period, strategies ranked by performance
- Metrics weighted:
  - 50% ROI (revenue / cost)
  - 25% Conversion rate
  - 15% Engagement quality
  - 10% Scalability potential

**3. Natural Selection**
- **Top 20% strategies:** Get 3x budget increase
- **Middle 60% strategies:** Maintain current budget
- **Bottom 20% strategies:** TERMINATED immediately

**4. Evolution Cycle**
- Winning strategies breed (variations created)
- Losing strategies die (budget reallocated)
- New mutations tested each cycle
- Continuous optimization loop

**Example:**

```
Week 1: Test 10 strategies @ $100 each
Week 2 Results:
  - TikTok Story Strategy: 500% ROI → 3x budget ($300)
  - Email Campaign A: 200% ROI → Maintain ($100)
  - Instagram Reels: 150% ROI → Maintain ($100)
  - Email Campaign B: 50% ROI → KILLED
  - Facebook Ads: 20% ROI → KILLED

Week 3:
  - TikTok Story Strategy: $300 budget
  - Create 2 variations of TikTok strategy
  - Email Campaign A: $100 budget
  - Instagram Reels: $100 budget
  - Test 4 NEW strategies @ $100 each
```

**Result:** Only profitable strategies survive and scale!
