## 🎓 KEY INNOVATIONS

1. **Strategies Die, Not Agents**
   - Traditional: Bad performers get fired
   - BLOOM: Agents evolve, bad strategies die

2. **Performance-Based Commission**
   - Traditional: Fixed salary
   - BLOOM: Top performers earn 1.5x more

3. **Collective Intelligence**
   - Traditional: Each learns independently
   - BLOOM: One learns, all benefit

4. **Natural Reproduction**
   - Traditional: Manual scaling
   - BLOOM: Automatic growth through success

5. **Protected Experimentation**
   - Traditional: All copy the winner (local maximum)
   - BLOOM: 70% copy, 30% experiment (global maximum)
